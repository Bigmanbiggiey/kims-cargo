# Project Brief

## 1. Overview

Build a professional, mobile-first website for **KIM'S & CARGO'S LOGISTICS COMPANY**, delivered by TECHBIGGIEY. The website should explain services and fleet, communicate coverage areas, and let prospective customers submit structured cargo quote enquiries with a requested date/time.

- Project reference: `TBG-KCLC-001`
- Baseline date: 26 October 2026
- Approved project build price: **KES 50,000**
- Initial delivery estimate: **15–20 working days**, subject to timely client content, approvals, access, and feedback.
- Maintenance option: **KES 5,000 every three months**, under a separately agreed service description.

## 2. Business goals

1. Present the company as credible and easy to contact.
2. Make services, fleet, and service coverage understandable on mobile and desktop.
3. Reduce back-and-forth by collecting the key information needed to prepare a quote.
4. Capture the customer's preferred collection/transport date and time without falsely promising vehicle availability.
5. Provide clear call and WhatsApp contact paths if the client supplies verified numbers.

## 3. Audience

Likely prospective customers include businesses and individuals seeking:
- Construction and industrial material haulage.
- Agricultural produce logistics.
- Closed-box commercial distribution.
- General freight and on-demand or contract haulage.

These are working audience assumptions to validate with the client.

## 4. Proposed information architecture

- Home
- About
- Services
- Fleet
- Service Areas & Routes
- Request a Quote
- Contact

The final page structure can be consolidated into fewer routes if the approved design/stack supports it, without dropping required content.

## 5. Fleet information currently supplied

- Mitsubishi Fuso FH closed-box lorry.
- Isuzu FRR 33 dropside lorry with canopy.

**Client verification required:** exact model year, payload, gross vehicle weight, dimensions, condition, photographs, and any claims about insurance, licensing, safety, or roadworthiness. Do not invent vehicle specifications.

## 6. Service coverage currently proposed

- Nairobi metropolitan area.
- Kitengela, Birika, Ongata Rongai, and Kajiado Town.
- Athi River, Syokimau, and Machakos Town.
- Thika, Juja, Ruiru, and Gatundu.
- Long-distance routes including Nairobi–Nakuru–Eldoret and Nairobi–Mombasa.

Publish only after the client confirms service availability. Coverage depends on cargo, route, scheduling, and operational capacity. Do not imply guaranteed service to every location.

## 7. Success measures

- Visitors can identify the company's services, fleet, and contact methods.
- The site works at common mobile and desktop widths.
- A visitor can submit a valid quote enquiry with a preferred date/time.
- The business receives the enquiry notification through the approved channel.
- The customer receives an appropriate acknowledgement if this is included in the final technical design.
- No false claim of live availability, confirmed booking, automatic price calculation, or payment completion.
- The client can own and access the domain, hosting, and critical accounts.

## 8. Constraints

- Total build budget is capped at KES 50,000 unless a written change is approved.
- Keep the solution maintainable and avoid unnecessary infrastructure.
- Domain, hosting, paid email delivery, map/provider usage, payment provider charges, and other recurring third-party charges are excluded unless explicitly agreed.
- Client supplies or approves content, photos, translations, business contacts, service areas, and legal/privacy wording.
